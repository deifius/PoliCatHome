Different material has different rights. The top-level BSD license does **not**
automatically license every image, third-party reference, submitted Cat, or
result produced by participants.

| --- | --- | --- |

| Project-authored documentation in `docs/`, `README.md`, `Philosophy.md`, `LICENSING.md`, and other original explanatory prose | **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** | [`docs/LICENSE-CC-BY-4.0.md`](docs/LICENSE-CC-BY-4.0.md) and the [official legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en) |

| Independently submitted Cats, their datasets, models, Docker images and outputs | **Creators/rightsholders specify their own licenses and terms**; contributors' PoliCs must also permit participation | Per-Cat manifest and declared rights |

copyright holders and contributors must not imply endorsement without
permission. The BSD 3-Clause text has **no explicit patent grant**.

This repository's code-level license does not impose a duty to publish
modifications, to join this Commons, or to donate compute.

## Documentation: CC BY 4.0

Original project-authored documentation can be shared and adapted, including
commercially, with appropriate attribution, a link to the license, and
indication of changes. CC BY 4.0 does not give rights to third-party work
included or cited in that documentation beyond the rights held by its licensor.

Suggested attribution: **“PoliC@home contributors, _PoliC@home documentation_,
CC BY 4.0”**, linking to the original project and
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Identify any modifications.

The code blocks in explanatory documents intended as runnable excerpts are
also offered under BSD 3-Clause insofar as the project owns the rights to them;
this lets users actually use the documented commands and sample code.

## Artwork and submitted Cats

No blanket grant is made for artistic works whose ownership, authorship,
source, or license has not been verified. In particular, references to James
Thurber, *The New Yorker*, the *Hackers* film, and older screensavers are
creative context, **not** inclusion of their assets or an affiliation claim.
Before adding independent illustrations, record creator, provenance, written
permission, and a chosen license in an asset-specific notice. Programmatic
WebGL/JS scene *source code* remains BSD-licensed as software.

The code for example Cats included under `projects/` is BSD-licensed as part
of this repository **where the project contributors hold the necessary rights**.
That does not set the license for Cats subsequently submitted by third parties.
Each submitted Cat must accurately state the rights for its code, inputs,
outputs and datasets; a PoliC declaration is consent for a computational
relationship, not a transfer of copyright. Nothing here automatically
assigns ownership of Cat results to the Commons or node owner.

## Publication checks

The `Copyright (c) 2026, PoliC@home contributors` notice identifies the
project's contributing authors collectively; it is not a copyright
assignment and does not resolve anyone's pre-existing ownership. Before
publishing publicly, verify that all authors/rightsholders of existing
material have authorized its release, and retain any third-party license
notices. A contributor cannot license code or artwork they do not control.

See also [Philosophy.md](Philosophy.md) and
[docs/art-and-rights.md](docs/art-and-rights.md).
